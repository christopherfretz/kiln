# WP-798 — bench regressions on main: bisected, fixed or justified

`cargo xtask bench` on main b1a2c66a7 reported 20 regressed entries against
`bench/baselines/*.json`. Each one is classified in
`docs/status/bench-regressions-WP-798.md` as real, expected, or noise/host,
with its first-bad commit. Three real regressions were fixed. Every other
entry was re-recorded under the rule this WP added to spec/bench.md
"Recording": no regression is re-recorded until it has been classified.

## Fixes

- **A failed trial fold gives its thunk's code back.** `kiln_oven/src/reclaim.rs`;
  spec/oven.md "Trial folds".
  - A trial fold (`try_fold`, `fold_rvalue`, `fold_for_overflow`) compiles a
    thunk and caches it. The thunk's code, sites and step locations were
    appended to flat tables that are never freed.
  - The `-Winteger-overflow` scan (WP-458) asks once per signed `+` operand.
    So a body of N operands that cannot be folded kept N thunks alive.
  - Now, when the failed thunk is the last thing the tables hold, they are
    cut back and the cache forgets the thunk.
  - Test: `crates/kiln_driver/tests/driver.rs`
    `failed_trial_folds_keep_no_thunks`. Before the fix it kept 200 thunks;
    now it keeps 1.
- **The overflow scan does not fold an operand that cannot fold.**
  `kiln_sema/src/int_overflow.rs` `overflow_fold_fails`; spec/sema.md §5.28.
  - The scan no longer folds an operand that calls a non-constexpr,
    non-virtual member function.
  - The Oven would refuse that call (`Fault::Callee`), so the fold could
    never succeed. The scan's diagnostics do not change.
  - Test: `crates/kiln_driver/tests/driver.rs`
    `overflow_scan_skips_folds_that_cannot_succeed`. Its one overflow
    warning is the one clang 19 gives for the same source.
- **-Wreturn-type builds no graph for a body ending in `return`.**
  `kiln_sema/src/stmt.rs` `falls_off`; spec/sema.md §7.5.
  - Control reaches the closing brace only through the last statement, so
    the answer is `Never` without a control-flow graph.
  - The graph costs 8 bytes per expression (WP-509).
  - Test: `tests/conformance/stmt.return/return-ending-body-no-cfg.cpp`.

Together on `overload/arrow-chain`: RSS 138.3 → 78.5 MB (baseline 77.3) and
827 → 538 ms (baseline 674).

## Rules

- spec/bench.md "Recording" now has the classification rule.
  - Time regressions against an old baseline are judged by an interleaved
    A/B with a build of the baseline commit.
  - RSS and counter regressions are bisected per commit.
- `budget-time` lines were not touched.
