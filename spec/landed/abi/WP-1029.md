# WP-1029 — `__extension__`, imaginary literals, pack indexing and member qualifiers in dependent signatures (closes WP-1030)

The WP-980 mangle differential found three expression kinds that kiln
mangled wrongly or not at all in dependent signatures
([temp.over.link]/5). The sibling sweep found that member-access
qualifiers were mangled wrongly as well. Every name below was checked
against clang 19 and clang 22. The summary rules are in spec/abi.md
§8.15, the profile rule in spec/types.md §7.2, and the parser rules in
spec/parse.md.

## `__extension__` (WP-1029)

- **AST:** the parser keeps `__extension__ e` as a `UnaryOperator`
  `UnaryOp::Extension`, as clang keeps `UO_Extension`. It used to drop
  the operator.
- **Transparency:** sema gives the node its operand's type, value
  category, bit-field and overload-set status. The Oven and codegen
  evaluate the operand. This covers constant initializers, case labels,
  template arguments, string-literal array initialization and
  `__imag__` of an imaginary literal, and `void` operands such as the
  `__extension__ ({ ... })` statement expressions in `arm_neon.h`
  (`ext.gnu.extension/extension-transparent-run.cpp`). Diagnostics on
  the operand are unchanged (`extension-transparent-errors.cpp`).
- **Mangling:** the node mangles as its operand. The operand is no
  longer an id-expression, so `decltype(__extension__ t)` is `DT`.
  - Before: `_Z3extIiEDtfp_ET_`.
  - After: `_Z3extIiEDTfp_ET_`.
  - `decltype(__extension__ g)` for a variable `g` is now `int &`, as in
    clang.

## Imaginary literals (WP-1030)

- **Rule:** clang's `ImaginaryLiteral` mangles as
  `L <complex type> <zero real> _ <imaginary> E`.
  - The zero real part is `0` for an integer element type, and the
    element format's width of hex zeros for a floating one.
  - `long double` is the 128-bit format on AArch64 and the 80-bit x87
    format on x86-64.
- **Before:** the error `cannot yet mangle expression type
  ImaginaryLiteral`.
- **After:**
  - `t + 2.0i` is `LCd0000000000000000_4000000000000000E`.
  - `2.0fi` is `LCf00000000_40000000E`.
  - `2i` is `LCi0_2E`.
  - `2.0li` is `LCe00000000000000000000_40008000000000000000E` on
    x86-64.
  - `__builtin_complex` operands are covered as well.
- **Test:** `tests/mangle/imaginary_literal.cpp`.

## Pack indexing (WP-1030)

- **Expressions:** a `PackIndexingExpr` writes nothing, in clang 19 and
  22 alike, and is a primary expression for the `X…E` rule.
  - `decltype(t...[0])` is `DTE`.
  - `t...[0] + 1` is `DTplLi1EE`.
  - Before: the error `cannot yet mangle expression type
    PackIndexingExpr`.
- **Types:** a `T...[i]` type mangles as its pattern while it is
  dependent, and as the selected type once it is substituted. It is a
  substitution candidate of its own, after the pattern.
  - `pt(T...[0], T...)`: before `_Z2ptIJiiEEvu7unknownDpT_`, after
    `_Z2ptIJiiEEvT_DpS0_`.
  - `-> T...[1]`: before `_Z3ptrIJidEEu7unknownDpT_`, after
    `_Z3ptrIJidEET_DpS0_`.
- **Template arguments:** `S<I...[0]>` is clang-22-only, since clang 19
  rejects it. It is `1SIE`, with no `X…E` wrapper
  (`_Z2paIJLi3ELi4EEEv1SIE`).
- **Parser:** `T...[0]{}` and `T...[0]()` are now functional casts
  ([expr.type.conv]). They used to be rejected with "unexpected type
  name".
- **Tests:**
  - `tests/mangle/pack_indexing.cpp`.
  - `tests/conformance/dcl.type.pack.index/`: `functional-cast-run`,
    `mangling-two-units-run`, and `template-arg-mangling-two-units-run`
    (`requires: clang>=22`).
- **Known gap:** `Y<1, 2>::g` with `-> decltype(u + I...[1])`, where
  `I` is the class's pack.
  - clang keeps the pack-index expression after the class's arguments
    are substituted: `_ZN1YIJLi1ELi2EEE1gIiEEDTplfp_ET_`.
  - kiln's substitution (`kiln_sema/src/templ/pack.rs`
    `pack_indexing_expr`) folds it to the selected element:
    `…DTplfp_Li2EET_`.
  - It is pinned by the xfail test
    `dcl.type.pack.index/substituted-expr-mangling-two-units-run.cpp`
    and left as a follow-up.

## Sibling sweep: member-access qualifiers

`tests/mangle/expression_sweep.cpp` covers 89 symbols in the clang
`mangle-exprs.cpp` style:

- unary, binary and assignment operators, `<=>`, `?:`, `[]`, `.*` and
  `->*`;
- calls, the five casts, functional and braced casts;
- member access, `sizeof`, `alignof`, `noexcept` and `typeid`;
- the `new` forms and the folds;
- character, string, integer, floating and `nullptr` literals;
- braced arguments and requires-expressions.

All of them already matched clang. The `throw` forms (`tw`) are in
`temp.over.link/throw-expression-two-units-run.cpp`, because the
differential compiles with `-fno-exceptions`. Three forms are not
covered, because clang cannot mangle them either: `__alignof__(expr)`,
`sizeof...` of a function parameter pack, and `__builtin_offsetof`.

The sweep found that kiln mangled the qualifier of a dependent member
access wrongly. The fixes are in `mangle/dep.rs`
`unresolved_prefix_in` and `unresolved_type_or_simple_id`, and the cases
are in `tests/mangle/member_qualifier.cpp`.

- **Spelled first level.** Behind an explicit object of dependent type,
  clang keeps an identifier nested-name-specifier: the first name is
  looked up later ([basic.lookup.qual.general]/2). The rule does not
  apply behind an implicit `this` or a `::` prefix.
  - `t.T::f()`: before `_Z3c17I1PEDTcldtfp_srT_1fEES1_`, after
    `_Z3c17I1PEDTcldtfp_sr1TE1fEET_`. There is no substitution for `T`.
  - `t.T::Self::f()`: before `srNT_4SelfE`, after `sr1T4SelfE`.
  - `t.U::m`: before `srT0_1m`, after `sr1UE1m`.
- **Typedef levels** keep their own name, as in clang's `Type::Typedef`
  case.
  - `t.A::Self::f()`: before `sr1P1PE`, after `sr1A4SelfE`.
  - `this->Base::m`: before `sr1PE1m`, after `sr4BaseE1m`.
- **Namespace aliases** keep their alias name: `t.na::P::f()` is
  `sr2na1PE`. Before, kiln wrote the namespace `2ns`.
- **Substituted template parameters** are unresolved types. In
  `C<P>`, `this->T::m` was `sr1PE1m` and is now `srS0_1m`.
- **Class template specializations** keep their arguments: `t.B<int>::f()`
  was `sr1BE1f` and is now `sr1BIiEE1f`.
- **Profile.** `CXXDependentScopeMemberExpr` with a dependent explicit
  object now profiles its qualifier as spelled, so `t.A::f()`,
  `t.P::f()`, `t.ns::P::f()` and `t.ns2::P::f()` are distinct
  (spec/types.md §7.2).
  - Before, the profile used the canonical qualifier, and later
    spellings took the first one's name.
  - `ast.spelled_member_qualifier` and `spelled_nns_words` are in
    `kiln_ast/src/types/profile_expr.rs`.
