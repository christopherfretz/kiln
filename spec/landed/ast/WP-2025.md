# WP-2025 — Using-declaration shadows and language linkage in the Ast

`Ast::using_shadows(u)` / `Ast::shadow_using(s)` (spec/ast.md §4.2) and
`Ast::language_linkage(d)` (§4.5) answer from the `Ast` alone, after the
translation unit, for tooling clients.

## Memory

The shadows are two write-once cold maps and one list per
using-declaration in the shared list arena. Linkage stores nothing.

Measured with `-fkiln-memory-report` on a C++26 `-fsyntax-only` TU that
includes `<algorithm>`, `<map>`, `<unordered_map>`, `<vector>`,
`<string>`, `<memory>`, `<functional>`, `<iostream>`, `<ranges>`,
`<format>`, `<variant>`, `<optional>`, `<tuple>`, `<chrono>` and `<regex>`
from libstdc++ 14 (114,305 declarations, 31.9 MB live):

| row | count | live | reserved |
|---|---|---|---|
| `decls.cold.using_shadows` | 525 | 4,725 B | 9,216 B |
| `decls.cold.shadow_using` | 1,133 | 10,197 B | 18,432 B |
| list arena (525 spans of 8 B, 1,133 ids of 4 B) | | 8,732 B | in the arena's growth |

Together that is about 23.7 KB live (27.6 KB reserved for the maps),
0.07% of the TU's 31.9 MB. Per using-declaration: 9 B in the forward
map, 8 B of span and 4 B per shadow in the list arena, and 9 B per
shadow in the reverse map. No hot structure grew: `DeclNode` and the
payload arenas are unchanged.

## Module images and snapshots

Each using-shadow record of a declaration image names its
using-declaration's record (IMAGE_VERSION 61, spec/modules.md §11.11
kind 21). The writer refuses an image (falling back to replay) when a
using-declaration's shadows are not carried in order, so an image never
loses the link. `crates/kiln_driver/tests/modules_image_using.rs`
compares the importer's map counts between the image and the replay. A
PCH snapshot carries both maps (snapshot format 127).
