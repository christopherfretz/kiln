# WP-873 — Driver/sema: -flto, CMake-probe flags, -Winvalid-constexpr at definition, Catch2 C++23/WERROR

Closes WP-673 (except its `lifetimebound` subject check, left to WP-674),
WP-677 and WP-687.

## `-flto` / `-flto=thin` (WP-673 item 1)
- Driver (`codegen_flags.rs`, `codegen_cmd.rs`; spec/driver.md "Codegen
  flags"): `-flto[=full|thin|auto|jobserver]`, `-fno-lto`,
  `-f[no-]split-lto-unit`. `opt` runs `lto-pre-link<Ox>` /
  `thinlto-pre-link<Ox>` (at `-O0` too). `-c` writes a bitcode-only `.o`
  (`opt -module-summary` for full LTO, `opt --thinlto-bc
  [--thinlto-split-lto-unit]` for thin); `-S -emit-llvm` carries the
  summary; the link gets `-flto[=thin]`.
- Codegen (spec/codegen.md §5.3): the `ThinLTO` / `EnableSplitLTOUnit`
  module flags and vtable `!type` metadata (address points, `.virtual`
  member-pointer ids, `distinct` ids for internal types), as clang 19.
- Evidence: `tests/codegen/lto_{full,full_o2,thin,thin_split}.cpp`
  (`ir_vs_clang`), `tests/conformance/lex.phases/lto-two-tu-*-run.cpp`
  (default linker and lld), `crates/kiln_driver/tests/codegen.rs`
  `print_commands_lto`; CMake `check_ipo_supported(LANGUAGES CXX)` with
  kiln++ as `CMAKE_CXX_COMPILER` reports YES, with and without
  `-fuse-ld=lld`.
- Not done: `-ffat-lto-objects` (accepted, bitcode only).

## WP-677 flags
`-fdiagnostics-show-template-tree`, `-f[no-]vectorize`,
`-f[no-]slp-vectorize`, `-f[no-]tree-vectorize`,
`-f[no-]tree-slp-vectorize`, `-f[no-]strict-vtable-pointers` are accepted
and ignored (spec/driver.md "Codegen flags" class 2). clang's
`-fstrict-vtable-pointers` adds `invariant.group` metadata and
`launder.invariant.group` barriers; kiln deliberately omits them (valid,
less optimized IR). The vectorizer flags change clang's pass pipeline
only; kiln keeps `opt`'s `-O`-level default.

## `-Winvalid-constexpr` at definition time (WP-673 item 3)
`kiln_sema/src/never_constant.rs`, spec/sema.md "Never constant": a port of
clang 19's `isPotentialConstantExpr` for functions, members, constructors,
destructors, conversions and explicitly `constexpr` lambdas (not
templates or instantiations). An error by default before C++23, an
off-by-default warning from C++23 that `-Winvalid-constexpr` turns on (as
an error). Nested constexpr calls are opaque, as clang's `CheckCallLimit`
(the oven's potential mode fails them with `Fault::Quiet`). Tests
(diagnostic-exact against clang 19 and 22):
`tests/conformance/dcl.constexpr/never-constant-body-cxx{20,23}.cpp`,
`never-constant-default-cxx23.cpp`, `never-constant-no-error-cxx20.cpp`.
False-positive checks: the corpus under kiln (oracle 22 and 19), the
libc++ M3 gate (642 = 636 pass + 6 unsupported) and Catch2 (C++20 and
`-Werror`).

## WP-673 item 2 (`no_sanitize*` / `lifetimebound` checks)
On main the `no_sanitize*` subject and argument checks match clang 19,
pinned by `tests/conformance/dcl.attr.grammar/sanitizer-attribute-subject-checks.cpp`.
`[[clang::lifetimebound]]` on a non-parameter is still accepted without
clang's error; WP-674 owns that code.

## Catch2 (WP-687)

(sharing the `catch2` checkout); both at parity with clang++-19
(docs/status/oss/catch2.md).
