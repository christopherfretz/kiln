# WP-792 — Driver: the -ftime-trace family

`-ftime-trace`, `-ftime-trace=<file>`, `-ftime-trace-granularity=<n>` and
`-ftime-trace-verbose` are accepted and ignored with "'-ftime-trace'
ignored: kiln writes no time trace (see -fkiln-instantiation-report)"
[-Wunused-command-line-argument] (`crates/kiln_driver/src/args.rs`,
`lib.rs`; spec/driver.md). glaze's test CMake adds `-ftime-trace` for
clang, and kiln rejected 81 TUs with "unknown argument". Test:
`crates/kiln_driver/tests/driver.rs`.
