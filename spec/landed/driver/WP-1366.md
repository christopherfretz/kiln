# WP-1366 — The codegen job returns the module; IR build and IR print are traced apart

Step K1 of the in-process back-end seam proposed by hod (its design note
`docs/design/0004-in-process-seam.md`). The driver now sees the built
`kiln_llvm::Module`; no back end is selected and nothing depends on hod.

## Deliverables

- **`CodegenJob::run` returns the module** (`crates/kiln_driver/src/codegen_cmd.rs`).
  `lower` drops the AST and the Oven before the module leaves it, and
  `run` drops the preprocessor and the source manager before returning it,
  so the caller's print (`print_ir`, which consumes the module) holds two
  of AST, module and text at its peak, as before (WP-633). After an error
  (a `-Werror` codegen warning, say) the module is dropped unprinted;
  nothing was written before either. `pch_check::codegen` prints each of
  its two modules once. The output is byte-identical.
- **Trace events** (spec/driver.md "Time trace"): `EmitIR` around
  `kiln_codegen::emit_with_debug`, and its sibling `PrintIR` around
  `Module::to_text`, both inside `Frontend`.
- **Evidence:** `crates/kiln_driver/tests/time_trace.rs`
  `emit_and_print_ir_are_siblings`; `crates/kiln_driver/tests/codegen.rs`
  `codegen_error_writes_no_output`.
- **Measured** (`-O0`, release build, 5 runs): printing is 5-16% of
  `EmitIR` + `PrintIR` and 0.5-9.5% of the whole compile, the high end
  with `-g` (the regex unit in `tests/conformance/re.alg/`, and
  `bench/frontend/trial-folds.cpp` and `registration-wide-obj.cpp`).
