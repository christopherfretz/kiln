# WP-706 — The Oven and contract assertions

Until WP-707 (design: spec/contracts.md §6.4), in
`crates/kiln_oven/src/compile.rs`:

- Under `ignore` a `contract_assert` and a function's `pre`/`post` are
  skipped; the evaluation goes on as without them.
- Under any checking semantic, compiling a call of a function with
  function contract specifiers, or a `contract_assert`, traps with the
  note `unsupported: contract evaluation (WP-707)` at the assertion, so
  the enclosing constant expression is not constant (no predicate is
  ever evaluated or assumed to hold).

Tests: `tests/conformance/basic.contract/oven-ignore.cpp`,
`oven-unsupported.cpp`, `oven-unsupported-observe.cpp`.
