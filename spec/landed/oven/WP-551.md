# WP-551 — reflection codebase compile profile

The contract for the new shapes is spec/bench.md "Reflection shapes".
The findings are in docs/status/perf-reflection.md. This file
records where the pieces live and the tests that cover them.

- **Shapes S7 and S8.** Both are generated by `cargo xtask gen-reflect`
  in `xtask/src/gen_reflect.rs` (`SHAPES`, `BindClass`, and the
  binding-library text). They are committed at N=40 as
  `bench/reflect/s7.cpp` and `s8.cpp`.
  - **S7** is the generator half. A consteval generator walks
    `members_of` over N annotated classes in a `template for` and builds
    each class's binding text as a `std::string`. A per-class
    `static_assert` checks the FNV-1a digest of that text. The budget is
    `peak_bytes<=64N+65536`.
  - **S8** is the production half. It is the rendered binding TU for the
    same N classes, compiled against a small header-only binding library.
    The budget is `peak_arena<=65536`.
- **libstdc++ `<meta>`.** S7 and S8 are the only shapes with
  `libstdcxx: true`. `gen_reflect::kiln_flags` adds `--gcc-toolchain`
  for them. Both `cargo xtask bench` (`xtask/src/bench/mod.rs`
  `measure`) and `--scaling` (`xtask/src/bench/scaling.rs`) pass these
  flags to kiln. S7 and S8 therefore carry no `xfail(kiln)` marker.
  S1–S6 keep theirs.
- **Flat-arena gate.** `scaling.rs` `FLAT_ARENA` now also lists S8, whose
  peak arena stays the same at every N.
- **No compiler change.** The WP adds no quick win. The report explains
  why and lists the proposed follow-ups.

Tests:

- `xtask/src/gen_reflect.rs`:
  - `deterministic_and_committed` checks that each shape's output is
    deterministic, that the committed files are current, and that only
    S1–S6 carry `xfail(kiln)`.
  - `s7_checks_what_s8_compiles` checks that the classes S8 binds are the
    classes whose digests S7 checks, and that S1 gets no extra flags.
- `xtask/src/bench/scaling.rs` `scaling_gate` covers the flat-arena gate.
- `cargo xtask bench bench/reflect` and `--scaling` run S7 and S8 against
  GCC 16.1.
