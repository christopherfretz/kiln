# WP-642 — Sublinear subobject lookups in the Oven

The rule is spec/oven.md "Offset index (WP-642)" under "Classes".
This file records where the change lives and the tests.

- **Problem.** Every by-offset lookup into a class scanned its field
  table: `Types::field_at`, `member_toward`, `extent_in`,
  `has_subobject`, `has_base_subobject` and `has_bit_unit` in
  `crates/kiln_oven/src/ty.rs`, and the fallbacks of `Vm::union_path`,
  `mutable_path` and `place_in_unions` in `crates/kiln_oven/src/vm.rs`.
  A store through a union member of a class with N members walks that
  path once per store, so N stores cost O(N^2):
  `bench/oven/wide-struct-store.cpp` grew 0.32 / 1.00 / 3.47 s at 4,096 /
  8,192 / 16,384 members (exponent 1.64, 1.79). The member access
  compiler (`Compiler::member` in `compile.rs`) also found a member's
  field index by a linear `position`, once per access expression.
- **Fix.** `Types::near(t, off)` yields the fields of `t` that may hold
  byte `off`, in declaration order, a superset of each caller's predicate,
  so every caller keeps its own first-match or greatest-offset rule.
  Fields are in declaration order, not offset order (a primary base,
  `[[no_unique_address]]` members, virtual bases), so a record with 16 or
  more fields gets a permutation sorted by `(offset, index)` and a prefix
  maximum of field ends, built once at layout time (`Types::index_fields`,
  called from `record_into` for the record and its base-subobject view).
  `near` binary-searches it and walks back while an earlier field may
  reach `off`; past 8 candidates or 32 steps it yields every field, the
  former scan. `Types::field_index` maps a member declaration to its
  field index. `Types::footprint` counts the type table, index included,
  in `Oven::footprint`.
- **Not changed.** `notes.rs` `union_field_at` (its predicate has no
  upper bound, so `near`'s superset does not cover it; it runs only
  when composing a diagnostic) and the `empty_bases` scan in
  `has_subobject` (empty bases are few).
- **Bench.** `bench/oven/wide-struct-store.cpp` (members holding a
  union, stored member by member) and `bench/oven/wide-struct-read.cpp`
  (reads only, no union), both in `bench/baselines/oven.json`. Numbers
  are in docs/status/perf-reflection.md "WP-642: sublinear
  subobject lookups".
- **Tests** (oracle clang 19 and 22, every class has at least 16 fields
  so the index is used):
  `tests/conformance/class.union/constexpr-wide-empty-base-same-offset.cpp`
  (a union activated through a nested subobject at the offset of empty
  bases), `class.union/constexpr-wide-bitfield-unit.cpp` (a shared
  bit-field unit, and a union of bit-field structs),
  `class.union/constexpr-wide-array-of-unions.cpp` (an array of
  union-holding structs stored element by element),
  `expr.const/constexpr-wide-empty-base-first-member.cpp` (an empty base
  at offset 0 under a non-empty first member), and
  `expr.const/constexpr-wide-offset-out-of-range.cpp` (pointers past a
  member, the last member and the object, diagnosed).
