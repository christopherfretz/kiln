# WP-644 — Subobject path cache in the Oven

The rule is spec/oven.md "Path cache (WP-644)" under "Classes".
This file records where the change lives and the tests.

- **Problem.** Every access to an allocation holding a union walks from
  the allocation's root type to the designated object (`Vm::union_path`,
  and `mutable_path`, `place_in_unions` and `extent_of` likewise in
  `crates/kiln_oven/src/vm.rs`), re-deriving at each level which member
  holds the object (`Types::member_toward`, `has_subobject`,
  `has_bit_unit`, `extent_in` in `crates/kiln_oven/src/ty.rs`). These are
  pure functions of (record, offset, type), and S7 repeats a handful of
  them hundreds of thousands of times: 794,167 `union_path` calls over 21
  distinct keys at N = 40, about 7% of S7's instructions (about 12% of
  each added class).
- **Fix.** `crates/kiln_oven/src/ty/path.rs`: `Types::hop` memoizes the
  memory-independent part of a walk from a record up to its next array
  or union (`Hop`), per (record, offset, type, walk). At a union it
  carries the mask of members that may hold the object, so the walk reads
  the active and possibly-active members from memory as before and only
  tests mask bits. `Types::extent` and `has_subobject` cache their answer
  for the first record below their leading arrays. Each of the three maps
  (`PathCache`) holds at most `PATH_CACHE_MAX` = 16,384 entries and is
  cleared by the next insert; `record_into` clears all three when it
  rebuilds a provisional slot. `Types::footprint` counts them. The fault
  paths (no possibly-active member holds the object) are composed
  uncached in `Vm::union_fault` and the `place_in_unions` error arm.
- **Bench.** S7 and S8 (`bench/reflect/`) and
  `bench/oven/wide-struct-{store,read}.cpp`; numbers are in
  docs/status/perf-reflection.md "WP-644: subobject path cache".
- **Tests** (oracle clang 19 and 22):
  `tests/conformance/class.union/constexpr-path-cache-activation.cpp`
  (one path walked through an inactive member, after activation and after
  reactivating another member),
  `class.union/constexpr-path-cache-array-of-unions.cpp` (one cached path
  into an array of unions with different active members),
  `class.union/constexpr-path-cache-two-roots.cpp` (an `int` at the same
  offset of two root types, one through a union), and
  `class.union/constexpr-path-cache-evict.cpp` (40,000 distinct paths,
  more than twice `PATH_CACHE_MAX`). Unit tests in `ty/path.rs` cover the
  clear-on-cap policy and the possibly-active member order.
