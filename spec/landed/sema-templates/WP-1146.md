# WP-1146 — member-template friend constraint tails

Follow-ups of WP-1130 (qualified member-template friends) and WP-1164
([temp.friend]/9). The rules are in spec/sema-templates.md, the
WP-1164 paragraph of "Friend templates" and the "Constraints" bullet of
the member-friend section.

- **Pattern friends are speculative** ([temp.friend]/5, [temp.constr.decl]/4).
  When a member friend's enclosing levels are not all known (the friend
  of a class template's pattern), `member_friend_constraints_match`
  answers "unknown" and the grant is `Grant::Speculative`: the access
  check is recorded and replayed at instantiation, where the levels are
  known (class/member_friend.rs). Every friend is instantiated from its
  original pattern with all its levels at once, so no friend is
  instantiated twice.
- **Requires-clauses that name function parameters** ([temp.over.link]/5,7):
  a parameter's canonical profile includes its type, so both sides'
  parameters are re-created with substituted types
  (`Substituter::nested_prototype_param`) before the requires-clauses are
  compared: `MemberFriend::fn_params` for the friend, the pattern's own in
  `member_friend_constraints_equivalent`, `member_cmp_constraints`
  (overload/redecl.rs) and `friend_cmp_constraints` (templ/member.rs).
- **[temp.friend]/9 decision:** `template<class T> friend int A<T>::w(..)
  requires C<T>;` inside a class template is a non-template friend with a
  requires-clause that is not a definition: rejected
  (`sema_non_temp_friend_decl_with_requires_clause_must_be_def` at the
  outermost head; at namespace scope `sema_trailing_requires_non_template`),
  as clang 19 and 22 do. `dependent-member-friend-constraints-overloads-run.cpp`
  dropped its `w` cases.
