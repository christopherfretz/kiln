# WP-632 — the lvalue-to-rvalue load of a reference cast of a reference-returning call

## Symptom

`sink(static_cast<const double&>(coeff()))`, with `coeff` returning
`const double&` and `sink` taking `double` by value, failed IR lowering
("call argument 0: expected double, found ptr") at every `-O` and `-std`;
the C-style, `const_cast`, nested-cast, `int` and member `operator<<`
spellings failed the same way, and `return static_cast<const double&>(f())`
silently stored the referee's address into the `double` return slot.
`sink(coeff())`, `static_cast<double>(coeff())` and a cast of a named
lvalue were correct.

## Root cause

Sema was right: `static_cast<const T&>(f())` is an lvalue of type
`const T` ([expr.static.cast]/4, [expr.cast]/4, [expr.const.cast]/3)
under an `ImplicitCastExpr <LValueToRValue>` ([conv.lval]/1), the same
tree clang builds. In codegen, `emit_scalar` of a reference-returning
call yields the referee's *address* (the lvalue), and the lvalue-to-rvalue
emitter (`Cg::emit_load_cast` → `Cg::emit_glvalue_load`) recognised such a
call only directly beneath it (or beneath a `CK_UserDefinedConversion`).
A glvalue `CK_NoOp` cast in between forwarded the call's address as its
scalar value, which then was never loaded.

## Rule

`Cg::emit_glvalue_load` (crates/kiln_codegen/src/expr.rs) looks through the
glvalues clang's `ScalarExprEmitter` visits through to their operand —
parentheses, an implicit or explicit glvalue `CK_NoOp` or
`CK_UserDefinedConversion` cast (`VisitCastExpr`), the right operand of a
glvalue comma (`VisitBinComma`, [expr.comma]/1) and the chosen operand of
`__builtin_choose_expr` (`VisitChooseExpr`) — and, when it reaches a
reference-returning call, emits the whole operand as an lvalue
(`emit_lvalue`) and loads it (clang `VisitCallExpr`: `EmitLoadOfLValue`).
A glvalue `CK_LValueBitCast` (`reinterpret_cast<const U&>(e)`,
[expr.reinterpret.cast]/11) on that path is also emitted as an lvalue and
loaded as `U` (clang `VisitCastExpr` `CK_LValueBitCast`), whatever its
operand; before, a named operand was loaded as its own type. The IR is a
call, a `load`, a call — identical to clang 19's and 22's at `-O0`, and
the TBAA of the `-O2` loads matches too.
