# WP-1113 — clang `annotate` codegen

The contract is spec/codegen.md "clang `annotate`". The sema side
(argument checking, evaluation, instantiation) is WP-954, spec/sema.md
§4.5.

## What landed

- `crates/kiln_codegen/src/annotate.rs` lowers the attribute in three
  forms: `@llvm.global.annotations` entries, `llvm.var.annotation` calls,
  and `llvm.ptr.annotation` calls. Hooks:
  - `emit_var_def` (module.rs) and the static-local path (expr.rs)
    annotate variables;
  - `get_fn` (module.rs) and `declare_structor` (class.rs) record
    functions;
  - the global trailer (global_init.rs) emits the array;
  - `emit_one_var_decl` (expr.rs) and the parameter prologue (func.rs)
    annotate locals and parameters;
  - `field_lvalue_tb` (expr.rs) annotates field accesses.
- `kiln_llvm::intrinsics::annotation` declares
  `llvm.{var,ptr}.annotation.p0.p0` with clang's attributes: `nocallback
  nofree nosync nounwind willreturn memory(inaccessiblemem: readwrite)`.
- **Sema:**
  - `annotate`'s non-first arguments are decayed and wrapped in a
    `ConstantExpr` that holds their value.
  - The parser reads a string-literal argument after the first as an
    expression, so `u8"s"` and `L"w"` are accepted, as clang accepts them.
  - An instantiated variable, local or variable template specialization,
    keeps `annotate` (`copy_attrs`, the `only_aligned` filter).

## clang versions

clang 19 and 22 emit the same IR for every form: the same intrinsic
names, `p0` suffixes and `ptr null` for no arguments. On a statement,
clang 22 emits nothing; clang 19 rejects it (sema).

## Deviations

- A function annotated only by a redeclaration that comes after its first
  use is entered when it is created, not when the redeclaration is seen.
  The set of entries is the same; their order can differ from clang's.
- clang's `annotate` is an inheritable parameter attribute: a parameter
  inherits it from an earlier declaration's. kiln annotates only the
  attributes written on the defining declaration's parameter.
- A VLA local (`emit_vla_var`) gets no `llvm.var.annotation`.
- `.args` sharing compares an aggregate argument by its bytes and
  relocations, rather than clang's per-field `APValue` profile. A pointer
  argument is compared by its base and offset, without its path.

## Tests

- `tests/codegen/annotate_global.cpp`, `annotate_local.cpp`,
  `annotate_field.cpp` and `annotate_template.cpp`: `ir_vs_clang` under
  oracles 19 and 22, on both triples.
- `crates/kiln_codegen/tests/lower.rs` `statement_annotate_emits_nothing`
  (at `-fclang-compat=22`). This cannot be a `tests/codegen` case: the
  harness lowers every case at the oracle's compat level, and clang 19
  rejects the attribute on a statement.
- `tests/conformance/dcl.attr/annotate-run-O2.cpp`: an `-O2` run.
- `tests/conformance/dcl.attr/annotate-encoded-string-arg.cpp`:
  encoding-prefixed string arguments are accepted.
