# WP-1003 — Source-based coverage mapping

The contracts are spec/driver.md "Coverage flags" and spec/codegen.md
§5.4 "Instrumentation". This file records what landed, how it was
validated, what it costs, and what was deferred. It follows
[WP-941](WP-941.md), which landed the counters this mapping refers to.

## What landed

- **Mapping regions** (`crates/kiln_codegen/src/coverage/mapping.rs`):
  clang 19 `CounterCoverageMappingBuilder`, ported statement for
  statement over the `pgo.rs` counters. It writes code, gap, branch,
  skipped and expansion regions, with clang's counter expressions and
  clang's region order. `EmptyCoverageMappingBuilder` gives an unused
  function its zero region.
- **Skipped regions** (`coverage/skipped.rs`): clang
  `CoverageSourceInfo`. The preprocessor reports its skipped groups and
  macro invocations (`kiln_pp::PpCallbacks::source_range_skipped`,
  `macro_expands`). kiln then rescans each file entry for comments and
  empty-line runs, modelling the tokens clang's watcher sees, so a
  range is trimmed or dropped exactly as clang's.
- **Encoding** (`coverage/encode.rs`, `coverage/records.rs`): version 6.
  - Each emitted function gets a `__covrec_<NameHash>` record in
    `__llvm_covfun` (`linkonce_odr hidden`, own comdat), carrying the
    NameHash, the pgo.rs FuncHash and the TU hash.
  - The TU gets `__llvm_coverage_mapping` in `__llvm_covmap`, with the
    filenames written uncompressed.
  - The names agree with `__llvm_prf_names` and pgo.rs.
  - Unused functions get zero records, named in `__llvm_coverage_names`.
  - `-fcoverage-compilation-dir=` and `-fcoverage-prefix-map=` are
    clang's `normalizeFilename`.
- **Driver:** the rejection is gone. `-fcoverage-mapping` sets
  `CoverageOptions::mapping`, and the preprocessor records what the
  mapping needs only when it is set. Without the flag the IR is
  WP-941's, byte for byte.
- **Locations fixed on the way.** These are clang's `getEndLoc`, and
  they also move diagnostic ranges toward clang's:
  - `Ast::end_loc` for `throw`, `co_await`/`co_yield`, and a
    `CXXConstructExpr` without parentheses or braces (its last
    non-default argument);
  - a mem-initializer's construct expression ends at its `)`/`}`
    (`kiln_sema` `note_mem_init_end`);
  - a named cast ends at its `)`.

## Validation

- **Encoded bytes.** clang is run with `-mllvm
  -enable-name-compression=false`, so that it writes the filenames
  uncompressed as kiln does. With that, the covmap, the TU hash and the
  `__covrec_` records are the same, byte for byte, on the programs
  below, apart from the listed deviations.
  - `ir_vs_clang` cases (exact at oracle 19 and 22, on both triples):
    `tests/codegen/coverage_mapping.cpp` (if/else, `?:` in a macro,
    every loop, switch fallthrough, try/catch, a template, unused inline
    and static functions, `= default`, a lambda) and
    `coverage_prefix_map.cpp`.
  - Sweep over 300 corpus programs (`-std=c++20 -fclang-compat=19`,
    the decoded records compared, sorted). 47 are rejected by clang
    (reflection, contracts) and 3 by kiln, with or without coverage. Of
    the other 250, 221 are the same, 4 differ only in system-header
    records and 25 differ. The sweep ran before the `= default` and
    constructor-end fixes, which removed several of those differences.
    The rest are the follow-ups below.
- **End to end, driver.** `crates/kiln_driver/tests/codegen.rs`
  `coverage_mapping_end_to_end` is one program with branches, loops, a
  macro, `#if 0`, a template, a lambda, a header function, a coroutine
  and unused functions. It compiles at `-fclang-compat=19` and `22`,
  links with clang++-19, runs, then runs that LLVM's `llvm-profdata
  merge` and `llvm-cov report/show`. Both modes report what clang 19
  reports (total, missed): regions 36, 7; functions 13, 3; lines 35,
  4; branches 16, 4.
- **End to end, comparison harness.** `scripts/coverage-compare.py`
  gained a `map` mode, which compares the `llvm-cov export
  -summary-only` totals per file and the `llvm-cov show` line counts.
  Two runs exit 0:
  - the default run, against clang++-19 with LLVM 19 tools;
  - `--mode map --llvm 22 --clang clang++-22 --link clang++-19`.

  The table is `llvm-cov` covered/total, and kiln equals clang 19 on
  every row except macros.cpp:

  - Against clang 22 there are two more KNOWN entries,
    `control_flow.cpp` and `macros.cpp`. They are clang 20+'s mapping,
    see the deviations below.

## Deviations from clang's encoding

- **Filenames are uncompressed.** clang zlib-compresses them, so the
  covmap bytes and the TU hash differ from a default clang build.
  `llvm-cov` reads both forms.
- **clang 20+ mapping is not ported.** kiln writes clang 19's mapping
  in every `-fclang-compat` mode. Against clang 20+, `llvm-cov` reports
  fewer regions:
  - clang 20+ adds gap regions between `&&`/`||` operands;
  - it gives real counters to folded-constant branch regions;
  - it reorders some branch counters;
  - it adds a region for a `do ... while (0)` macro body.
- **Missing zero records.** clang adds every function-template
  specialization and every class-template member instantiation to its
  deferred list (`HandleTopLevelDecl`). Some of these kiln instantiates
  only for constant evaluation, and those get no zero record in kiln.
  A specialization used only in a `static_assert` is therefore absent
  from kiln's report, where clang shows it at 0%.
- **Generic lambdas.** Their call operators get extra kiln records:
  hash-24 `u` records, and zero records for some immediate-invocation
  helpers.
- **Duplicate zero-length regions.** At expansions of system-header
  NEON macros, kiln writes zero-length regions twice.
- **Location details.** A skipped range after an init-capture ends 2
  columns late. A qualified base initializer `D9::B(4)` starts at `B`.
  A compound literal `(long){}` ends early.
- **Lambda names.** Local lambdas' `$_N` numbers differ from clang's,
  as in WP-941, so their NameHash differs. The regions are the same.

## Cost

fmt 12.1.0 `test/format-test.cc` was measured with WP-941's method and
command line: `/usr/bin/time -f "%e %M"`, best wall time of 3 runs and
largest peak RSS. kiln is the `harness` profile build at
`-fclang-compat=19`, and the oracle is clang++-19. The run was on the
shared aarch64 host with other jobs running. "IR" is `-S -emit-llvm
-Xclang -disable-llvm-passes`.

    $cc [-fclang-compat=19, kiln only] -DGTEST_HAS_STD_WSTRING=1 \
        -I fmt/include -isystem fmt/test/gtest -O0 <mode> -std=gnu++20 \
        -fvisibility=hidden -fvisibility-inlines-hidden \
        -c fmt/test/format-test.cc -o f.o

| compiler, mode | wall s | peak RSS KB | object bytes | IR bytes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| kiln, none | 3.90 | 147276 | 4818528 | 12962634 |
| kiln, `-fprofile-instr-generate` | 4.16 (+7%) | 168800 (+15%) | 6639280 | 14576312 |
| kiln, `-fprofile-instr-generate -fcoverage-mapping` | 4.48 (+15%) | 172992 (+17%) | 7297056 | 15757312 (+22%) |
| clang, none | 3.70 | 329012 | 4809264 | 14946418 |
| clang, `-fprofile-instr-generate` | 3.72 (+1%) | 332368 (+1%) | 6590632 | 16441136 |
| clang, `-fprofile-instr-generate -fcoverage-mapping` | 4.84 (+31%) | 347800 (+6%) | 7527920 | 18342309 (+23%) |

On top of the counters, the mapping costs kiln 0.32 s of wall time
(+8%), 4 MB of peak RSS (+2.5%) and 1.2 MB of IR (+8%). clang pays 1.12
s and 15 MB for the same step. With coverage, kiln's peak is still
half of clang's peak without it.

## Follow-ups

None of these is a breakage: no valid TU is rejected, nothing crashes
and the program behaves the same. No xfail repro was added.

- clang 20+ mapping under `-fclang-compat=22`:
  `crates/kiln_codegen/src/coverage/mapping.rs:1472` (`logical`, gaps
  between operands), `:598` (`branch`, folded counters). Its gate is the
  `KNOWN` entries tagged `"22"` in `scripts/coverage-compare.py`.
- Zero records for constant-evaluation-only instantiations: they must
  also be deferred where kiln instantiates them
  (`crates/kiln_codegen/src/coverage/records.rs:116`
  `cov_defer_unused`; `crates/kiln_codegen/src/module.rs:735`).
- The `#x` stringizing as a nested expansion
  (`crates/kiln_codegen/src/coverage/mapping.rs:60`). Its gate is the
  `("macros.cpp", "map")` `KNOWN` entry.
- Generic-lambda and immediate-invocation extra records
  (`crates/kiln_codegen/src/coverage/records.rs:238` `cov_add_record`).
- Duplicate zero-length expansion regions at system-header macros
  (`crates/kiln_codegen/src/coverage/mapping.rs:1659`).
- Skipped-range end after an init-capture
  (`crates/kiln_codegen/src/coverage/skipped.rs:113`). The qualified
  base-initializer start is in `crates/kiln_sema/src/class/ctorinit.rs:92`.
  The compound-literal end is in `crates/kiln_ast/src/ast.rs:107`
  (`end_loc`).
- Linking `-fprofile-instr-generate` with kiln's default LLVM 22 needs
  LLVM 22's `libclang_rt.profile`, which this container lacks. Link with
  `--llvm-dir=/usr/lib/llvm-19` or clang++-19 (environment, not code).
