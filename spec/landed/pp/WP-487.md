# WP-487 — the `pp/headers` regression (O(1) attribute-macro history), the bench `budget-time:` directive, the libc++ slice's clang-20 floor

Extends spec/pp.md "Performance obligations" and spec/bench.md
"Directives" and "The libcxx-slice entry".

## The regression and its cause

`cargo xtask bench` row `pp/headers` (`-E` on `bench/pp/headers.cpp`)
went from 866 ms (2026-10-28, `83ba262d0`) to 2443 ms (2026-11-03).
`git bisect run --first-parent` over the 657 first-parent commits, on the
self-recursive half of the entry (the libc++-include half moved 347 to
374 ms), found `203242dcd` (PR #27, WP-234): `note_macro_change`, which
keeps the attribute-macro history for `-Wimplicit-fallthrough`'s fix-it
(`#define X [[fallthrough]]`), scanned the whole history on every
`#define`/`#undef` once one attribute macro existed. The entry re-enters
itself 5120 times and redefines `HDR_NO_UNIQUE [[no_unique_address]]` and
`HDR_LIKELY [[likely]]` each pass, so the history reached ~10k entries and
the scan was quadratic. Header search, `#pragma once`, the guard detector
and the predefines were measured and are not involved.

## The fix (`crates/kiln_pp`)

- `Preprocessor::attr_live` (`SymMap<usize>`) maps each name whose
  attribute-macro definition is in effect to its `attr_macros` index.
  `note_macro_change` closes that entry through the map (O(1)) and
  returns at once when the directive defines no attribute macro and no
  entry is live. The history's contents and `attr_macro_gen` are as
  before: one entry per attribute-macro definition, `end` set by the
  next `#define`/`#undef` of the name ([cpp.scope]/1).
- Result at one load (min of 3, load ~22): 1290-1459 ms against
  1234-1276 ms for `83ba262d0` and 3180 ms for main before the fix; clang
  783-933 ms. Under callgrind a reduced 80-pass variant runs 148.6M
  instructions against 139.2M at `83ba262d0` and 152.8M before the fix;
  the residue is diffuse (`Lexer::next` +4.2M since PR #23 WP-143's
  comment recording, +1.4M startup), not one hotspot.

## `budget-time: <ms>` (`xtask/src/bench`)

A bench entry directive: kiln's fastest run must take at most `<ms>`
(a positive, finite number; a duplicate is an error). It is checked on
every kiln run, `--record` included, so a re-recording cannot absorb a
slowdown past it; a breach fails the entry (`over the time budget: ...`,
exit 1) and the entry is not recorded. Other compilers are not held to
it. `bench/pp/headers.cpp` carries one at 1.5x its recorded kiln median.

## The libc++ slice's clang-20 floor

With `bench/libcxx-slice` selected, a clang `--compare-with` or
`--compiler` older than clang 20, or one whose `--version` names no
version, is refused before any setup (`slice::check_clang_floor`, exit
2): libc++ 22 rejects older clangs under the runner's `-Werror`, so every
test would fail. kiln and other kinds are not held to it; without the
slice clang 19 stays a valid oracle.

## Other `pp/*` rows

None is over 1.5x clang (see `docs/status/perf.md` "WP-487"), so none
gets a diagnosis.

## Tests

- `crates/kiln_pp/tests/hooks.rs` `cpp_scope_attr_macro_history`: the
  history's entries and ranges across redefinition, `#undef`, a
  non-attribute redefinition and an unrelated macro ([cpp.scope]/1);
  `cpp_scope_attr_macro_history_churn`: 2000 passes of two attribute
  macros, 4000 entries, one live, the generation counter.
- `xtask/src/bench/entry.rs`: `budget-time` parsing, defaults and
  rejections (`0`, negative, non-numeric, `inf`, `NaN`, duplicate).
- `xtask/src/bench/tests.rs` `time_budget_with_a_fake_kiln`
  (`xtask/fixtures/bench/budget/`): a breach fails with and without
  `--record` and is not recorded, a run inside the budget passes, clang
  is not held to it; `slice_refuses_an_oracle_older_than_clang_20`.
- `xtask/src/bench/slice.rs` `refuses_clang_older_than_20`.
