# WP-538 — traits from a variable template initializer instantiated by `std::meta::substitute`

The rules are spec/reflection.md §4.3 ("A Transactional checkpoint alone
is not a trial for the bridge"), spec/reflection-traits.md §2.3,
spec/oven.md "Trial evaluation" and spec/sema-templates.md §4.11.

- **Cause.** `substitute_uncached` (`crates/kiln_sema/src/meta/templ.rs`)
  opens `Oven::trial_begin` (a `TrialMode::Transactional` checkpoint)
  around `act_template_id`. For a variable template that instantiates the
  specialization and evaluates its constexpr initializer on the lent
  Oven. The VM computed `MetaCtx::trial` as `run.fold || mem.in_trial()`
  (`crates/kiln_oven/src/vm/meta.rs`), so every metafunction call in that
  initializer saw a trial, and the heavy traits (constructible,
  assignable, destructible: `meta_trait`, `meta_trait_range` in
  `crates/kiln_sema/src/meta/traits.rs`) returned `MetaError::silent()`:
  "must be initialized by a constant expression" with no note. A direct
  `V<^^T>` or a class template's static member (evaluated after
  `substitute` returns) never saw the checkpoint.
- **Fix.** `MetaCtx::trial` is `run.fold || mem.in_speculative_trial()`
  (`Memory::in_speculative_trial`, `crates/kiln_oven/src/mem.rs`): the
  Transactional checkpoint makes memory effects undoable but the
  evaluations under it are real. `Mutating` ops are still refused under
  any checkpoint (a rollback cannot undo a class definition), now with a
  note when no trial hides it. The substitution keeps its SFINAE frame
  and its rollback (`reflection/substitute-trial-rollback.cpp` unchanged).
- **Notes.** A heavy trait refused in a fold or a speculative trial
  carries "'X' is not evaluated during a speculative evaluation (it may
  instantiate)" (dropped with the trial's notes), and the VM adds
  "kiln: internal: 'X' failed without a reason" to any noteless bridge
  failure outside a trial, so the bare failure cannot recur silently.
- **Tests** (`tests/conformance/meta.reflection.substitute/`, all
  `oracle: gcc`, strict-diags): `substitute-var-template-traits.cpp` (the
  issue's repro and every constructible trait, a class template operand
  instantiated by the query), `substitute-memo-door-nested.cpp` (a door
  whose initializer opens a second door),
  `substitute-var-template-assign-destroy.cpp` (the assignable and
  destructible families, true and false answers),
  `substitute-var-template-traits-neg.cpp` (an incomplete operand: the
  "invalid use of incomplete type" note).
- **Follow-up.** GCC 16.1 accepts `define_aggregate` from a consteval
  block of a class template that a `substitute`-instantiated variable
  template's initializer instantiates; kiln refuses it (the `Mutating`
  gate under the Transactional checkpoint, and `Oven::plain` is false
  under any checkpoint).
