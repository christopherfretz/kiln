# WP-607 — `throw`/`try` under `-fno-exceptions`: suppressed in system headers, deferred in templates

[expr.throw], [except.pre], [temp.res.general], [temp.inst]. The rule is
in `spec/sema.md` §5.5, "Exceptions disabled".

- With `-fno-exceptions`, every TU that included libstdc++'s `<exception>`
  failed: the never-instantiated `std::throw_with_nested`
  (bits/nested_exception.h) holds a raw `throw`, and kiln reported
  "cannot use 'throw' with exceptions disabled" at its definition.
- `Sema::check_exceptions_enabled` (except.rs), shared by `act_on_throw`
  and `act_on_try`, now skips a location in a system header (clang's
  rule) and, inside a template definition (`in_template()`), waits for the
  instantiation (GCC's rule). A rebuilt node re-runs the check through
  `act_on_throw` / `act_on_try` in the substitution; a non-dependent
  throw-expression the substitution shares is recorded in
  `Sema::exc_disabled_pattern` and reported by `note_shared_throws`
  (templ/subst/exprs.rs). Each location is reported at most once
  (`Sema::exc_disabled_seen`).
- Codegen needed no change: a surviving `throw` is the plain
  `__cxa_allocate_exception` / `__cxa_throw` call sequence and a surviving
  `try` its compound statement, byte-identical to clang 19 and 22.
- Tests: `tests/conformance/expr.throw/no-exceptions-include-exception.cpp`,
  `no-exceptions-pragma-system-header.cpp`, `no-exceptions-template.cpp`,
  `no-exceptions-template-uninstantiated.cpp` (GCC oracle),
  `no-exceptions-throw-with-nested-run.cpp`;
  `tests/conformance/except.pre/try-no-exceptions-system-header.cpp`,
  `try-no-exceptions-template.cpp`; `tests/codegen/throw_no_exceptions.cpp`.
- Divergence from clang (pinned `skip(clang)`): clang 19/22 diagnose a
  template's `throw`/`try` at its definition; kiln accepts a
  never-instantiated one, as GCC does.
