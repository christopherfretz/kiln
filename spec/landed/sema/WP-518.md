# WP-518 — lambda init-captures with direct and braced initializers, and the WP-123 lambda rows

Extends spec/sema-lambda.md §1, §2, §4.1, §4.3, §9, §10 and §11.3. Clang
22 is the oracle; clang 19 agrees on every case below.

## Init-captures ([expr.prim.lambda.capture]/6, /17)

- Parser (`kiln_parse/src/lambda.rs`, `parse_init_capture_initializer`):
  `= e`, `= {...}`, `(e...)` and `{...}`, with the `&` and `...`
  prefixes, into `LambdaCaptureSpec.init: Option<(InitStyle,
  Initializer)>`, the simple-declaration initializer representation.
  `[x()]` is `expected expression`; a failed initializer becomes an error
  expression and the capture is dropped quietly.
- Sema (`lambda/capture.rs`, `add_init_capture`): the variable is created
  at the introducer, deduced by `deduce_var_auto` as `auto x <init>`
  (`[x{1}]` int, `[x = {1}]` `std::initializer_list<int>`,
  `[x{1, 2}]` / `[x(a, b)]` ill-formed) and initialized by
  `perform_init` with direct-initialization for `(e)` / `{e}` (explicit
  constructors considered) and copy-initialization for `= e`.
  `[&r(x)]` binds a reference. Errors use clang's `err_init_capture_*`
  wording (`sema_init_capture_*` ids, selected by the `entity::CAPTURE`
  initialization entity).
- Placeholder variables (`templ/auto.rs`, `decl.rs`): the expression
  count of a parenthesized / direct-list initializer is checked before
  type-dependence (`auto x(t, t);` is diagnosed in the template
  definition, as clang), and `auto x({1});` / `auto x{{1}};` are
  `cannot deduce type for variable ... from parenthesized|nested
  initializer list` (`sema_auto_var_init_paren_braces`, also when
  dependent).
- Templates (`templ/subst/lambda.rs`, `init_capture_init`): the
  initializer is rebuilt in the pattern variable's init style and
  replayed through `add_init_capture`; packs (`[...xs(ts)]`,
  `[&...rs{ts}]`) expand per element.
- Oven and codegen needed no change: the closure member's initializer is
  the variable's (an `InitListExpr`, the bare expression, or a
  `CXXConstructExpr`); `-O0` IR matches clang 19 and 22
  (`tests/codegen/lambda_init_capture_forms.cpp`).

## WP-123 rows

Verified working, positive tests only: `consteval` lambdas and immediate
escalation (`consteval-lambda.cpp`), a lambda as a template argument and
an `auto` NTTP whose default is a closure (`template-argument.cpp`, no
xfail since WP-117), `x.template operator()<T>()` on a generic lambda
(`template-operator-call.cpp`).
