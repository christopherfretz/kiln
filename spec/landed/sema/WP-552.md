# WP-552 — Template substitution of resolved overloaded operators (`operator->`)

Extends spec/sema.md §5.21 ("Resolved operator calls at instantiation").
Clang 19 and 22 and GCC 16.1 accept every case below.

## `->` through `operator->` in a template ([over.ref]/1, [temp.res.general]/6)

- `opt->m()` with a non-dependent `std::unique_ptr` / `std::shared_ptr`
  / map-iterator operand inside a function template reported "kiln: not
  yet implemented: this overloaded operator" at instantiation. The
  member-access rebuild (`templ/subst/exprs.rs` `MemberExpr`, and
  `act_on_member_template_args` in `templ/funcs.rs`) stripped the
  `operator->` chain only when the calls were directly nested, so the
  qualification conversion of the returned pointer that a `const` member
  function adds (`Opt *` → `const Opt *`) left the `operator->` call as
  the base, and its `CXXOperatorCallExpr` arm had no mapping for `->`.
  The chain is now stripped through implicit casts, materialized and
  bound temporaries (`Sema::arrow_chain_object`, `overload/arrow.rs`),
  and a lone `operator->` call (a pseudo-destructor `p->~I()` through a
  class) is rebuilt as one link by overload resolution on the substituted
  object (`Sema::rebuild_operator_arrow`).
- Audit of the other operators `unary_of`/`binary_of` cannot map: `->*`
  and `,` already map to `PtrMemI` / `Comma` (now tested); `new`/`delete`
  never form a `CXXOperatorCallExpr`; `operator co_await` is rebuilt from
  the `co_await` operand as written.
