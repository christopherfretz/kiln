# WP-638 — Instantiate a variable's definition when a reflection reads it; S1–S6 on libstdc++

Extends spec/reflection.md §3 ("Reading a variable through a reflection")
and spec/bench.md "Reflection shapes".

`bench/reflect/s5.cpp` on libstdc++ failed with "initializer of 'value' is
unknown": `extract<int>` of a static data member of a specialization that
`substitute` produced (reached through `static_data_members_of`, never
named) read a member whose deferred in-class initializer
([temp.inst]/3) no expression had instantiated. `extract`, `constant_of`
and `object_of` now call `Sema::use_reflected_var`
(crates/kiln_sema/src/meta/mod.rs) first, which runs the odr-use path of a
spelled reference (`note_var_referenced`: the deferred initializer, the
out-of-line definition, a variable template specialization), potentially
evaluated whatever the surrounding context, with the Oven lent back
(R-OVEN-1). `type_of`, `is_const` and the other declaration queries do
not instantiate the initializer, so an ill-formed one stays reflectable;
reading it reports the error with the "in instantiation of static data
member" note at the reading call.

Bench: `xtask/src/gen_reflect.rs` generates S1–S6 with `libstdcxx: true`
(no `xfail(kiln)`, `--gcc-toolchain` from `kiln_flags`); their headers say
"Exercises <owner> and <families>" (each shape carries its specifying
section in `Shape::source`). Numbers: docs/status/perf-reflection.md.

Tests: `tests/conformance/meta.reflection.substitute/substitute-static-member-init.cpp`,
`tests/conformance/meta.reflection.extract/static-member-instantiate.cpp`,
`tests/conformance/meta.reflection.extract/static-member-instantiate-neg.cpp`
(all `oracle: gcc`); `xtask` `gen_reflect::tests::deterministic_and_committed`.
