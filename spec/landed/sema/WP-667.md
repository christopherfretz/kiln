# WP-667 — clang's sanitizer and lifetimebound attributes are known and ignored

Found building fmt 12.2.0 with kiln++ (docs/status/oss/fmt.md): its
`reserve` carries `__attribute__((no_sanitize("undefined")))` and its
`join` parameters `[[clang::lifetimebound]]`, both under
`__has_attribute` / `__has_cpp_attribute` checks that kiln's feature
table already answered 1 for. kiln warned `unknown attribute 'x'
ignored` [-Wunknown-attributes] on every use (48 warnings in the fmt
build, none under clang).

## Behaviour

- [dcl.attr.grammar]/4, /6 (spec/sema.md §4.5, "Known and ignored, no
  diagnostic: sanitizer and lifetime attributes"): table
  `IGNORED_CLANG_ATTRS` in `decl_attrs.rs`, checked in
  `attribute_node` right after the thread-safety family. `no_sanitize`,
  `no_sanitize_memory` and `lifetimebound` are known as
  `__attribute__((x))` and `[[clang::x]]`; `no_sanitize_address`,
  `no_sanitize_thread` and `no_address_safety_analysis` as
  `__attribute__((x))` and `[[gnu::x]]`; `[[gnu::no_sanitize]]` from
  `-fclang-compat=22` (clang 22 added the spelling; clang 19 warns).
  Each attaches nothing and gets no subject or argument check. Other
  spellings stay unknown attributes, as in clang.
