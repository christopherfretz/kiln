# WP-557 — carry builtins __builtin_addc*/__builtin_subc*

Extends spec/sema-templates.md §9.5 (the builtin table, "carry" row),
spec/oven.md "Builtins" (checked arithmetic) and spec/codegen.md §3.

clang's multiprecision arithmetic builtins (`LanguageExtensions.rst`
"Multiprecision Arithmetic Builtins"), for `X` = `b`, `s`, (none), `l`,
`ll` and `T` = `unsigned char`, `short`, `int`, `long`, `long long`:

    T __builtin_addcX(T x, T y, T carryin, T *carryout);
    T __builtin_subcX(T x, T y, T carryin, T *carryout);

are declared (`builtin::CHECKED`, kiln-private ids 2160-2169, signatures
`UcUcUcUcUc*` .. `ULLiULLiULLiULLiULLi*`; ordinary parameter conversion, so
a mismatched carry-out pointer is "cannot initialize a parameter of type"),
staged on (`__has_builtin` and `__has_constexpr_builtin` answer 1, as
clang 19), constant-evaluated (`bi::CARRY`: two wrapping steps, the carry
the OR of their overflows; the carry-in is any value of `T`) and lowered
as two `llvm.u{add,sub}.with.overflow` calls with the flags OR-ed and
zero-extended into `*carryout`. Formatting libraries that guard
`__builtin_addcll` with `__has_builtin` (a two-limb 128-bit fallback
`operator+=`) now compile.

Tests: `tests/conformance/builtin.carry/` (`carry-run.cpp`,
`carry-constexpr.cpp`, `carry-has-builtin.cpp`, `carry-args.cpp`,
`carry-uint128-run.cpp`), `tests/codegen/builtins_carry.cpp`, and the
`carry_rows` unit test of `kiln_sema::templ::builtins::table`.
