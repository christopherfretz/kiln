# WP-549 — immediate invocations inside a constant-initialized variable's initializer are validated by that check

Extends spec/sema.md §5.10 ("Invocations validated by constant
initialization"). GCC 16.1 accepts every accepted case below; clang 19
and 22 accept all but the in-class static data member, and clang 19 also
rejects a variable template whose call has a value-dependent argument.

## Constant initialization validates the call ([expr.const.imm]/1.2, [expr.const.defns]/1.6, [expr.const.init]/4)

- `constexpr D e = flat().d;` with `consteval P flat()` and `P` owning
  heap through a member was rejected ("call to consteval function 'flat'
  is not a constant expression" / "pointer to subobject of
  heap-allocated object"): each outermost invocation was checked on its
  own, and for a constexpr/constinit variable `report_deferred_immediates`
  re-evaluated and reported it even when the variable had just been
  constant-initialized. It now drops the deferred invocations when the
  variable has a value (constexpr) or `check_constinit` succeeded
  (constinit), and still reports them after the variable's own error
  otherwise (the WP-465 order). Covers namespace-scope, local constexpr
  in a constexpr function, static constexpr data members (GCC's
  behaviour) and variable template specializations.
- A static-storage variable that is neither constexpr nor constinit
  (`D g = flat().d;`, also a local `static`) skips the individual report
  when its initializer folds: it is then constant-initialized.
- Variable templates whose initializer is shared with the pattern
  reported the failure twice (at the definition and again from
  `recheck_shared_invocation` at the specialization). The re-check is
  now deferred to the specialization's own check (`note_var_init_subst`
  in `templ/subst/decls.rs` `var_init`, `ImmPending::shared_var`).
- The instantiated initializer of a pattern with cleanups is rebuilt
  from the `ExprWithCleanups` operand (clang's `TransformInitializer`),
  so the temporary of `template<class T> constexpr T v = q().d;` is no
  longer destroyed inside the conversion to `T` ("read of object
  outside its lifetime" before).

Calls outside such an initializer (a return statement, a constexpr
function body, an automatic variable, a static-storage variable whose
initializer is not constant) are still checked on their own, with
clang's diagnostics.

Tests: `tests/conformance/expr.const/immediate-invocation-constant-init*.cpp`,
`tests/conformance/temp.inst/variable-template-dependent-type-temporary-member.cpp`.
