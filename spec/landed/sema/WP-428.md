# WP-428 — the layout of a class being defined, instantiated out-of-line definitions at their pattern definition

- **Layout of a class being defined** (spec/sema-class.md §6.5 "Layout
  caches", spec/abi.md §1). A class's implicit virtual destructor is
  declared, and its deletion decided ([class.dtor]/7.3), before `}`. Its
  deallocation function is selected by the class's alignment
  ([expr.delete]/10, [class.dtor]/16; `Sema::complete_class_align`,
  `crates/kiln_sema/src/new_delete.rs`). That class is now laid out over
  `Sema::layout_cache`, reusing the cached layouts of its complete bases
  and members. The session then ends with `Layouter::into_complete_cache`,
  which drops every class it laid out while incomplete. Before this
  change, the class was laid out from scratch with all of its bases (WP-450
  cached complete classes only). That was cubic in the length of a chain
  of polymorphic classes, and half of a deep virtual lattice's compile.
  The Oven's and codegen's layouts were already shared through
  `Ast::layouts` (`ensure_layout`). Sema's `sizeof`/`alignof` only check
  their operand. Tests: `kiln_abi` `into_complete_cache_drops_an_incomplete_class`;
  `bench/frontend/virtual-dtor-chain.cpp` and its baseline (600 classes:
  3.8 s before, 0.28 s after, clang 22 1.1 s);
  `tests/stress/virtual-lattice-deep.cpp`. Numbers are in
  docs/status/perf-history.md "WP-428".
- **An instantiated out-of-line definition is located at its pattern
  definition** (spec/sema-templates.md §4.6, sema-class.md §4.4). When a
  member function's definition is instantiated from an out-of-line pattern
  definition, whether a body or `= default`, the specialization takes that
  definition's location and declarator end
  (`AstBuilder::relocate_to_pattern_definition`; clang
  `InstantiateFunctionDefinition`). This applies to errors in the
  instantiated definition, such as a deleted, inaccessible or missing
  subobject member, or a reference member not initialized. It also applies
  to the "in defaulted ... first required here" note, and to later candidate
  and "declared here" notes. All of these now match clang 22 and clang 19,
  which agree here. Tests:
  `tests/conformance/dcl.fct.def.default/instantiated-out-of-line-loc.cpp`,
  `tests/conformance/temp.inst/out-of-line-definition-location.cpp`.
- **Where a defaulted assignment operator is defined** ([class.copy.assign]/12;
  clang `DefineImplicitCopyAssignment` uses `getEndLoc()`). An
  instantiation of a member's `operator=` is requested at the declaration's
  end, which `ColdDeclData::declarator_end` (spec/ast.md) now also records
  for `= default`/`= delete` definitions:
  - out of line, the declarator's closing `)`;
  - in a class, the last character of `default` (clang
    `ParseCXXInlineMethodDef` `setRangeEnd(KWEndLoc)`);
  - for an implicit declaration, the class.

  An instantiated out-of-line `= default` skips the deletion checks, so a
  member's deleted `operator=` is `attempt to use a deleted function` with
  the "explicitly marked deleted here" note ([dcl.fct.def.delete]/2). It
  was an "is not supported yet" error. Test:
  `tests/conformance/dcl.fct.def.default/assign-instantiation-point.cpp`.
