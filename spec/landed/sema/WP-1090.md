# WP-1090 — variadic special members and the pre-C++20 aggregate rule

WP-1040 found that kiln treated `struct s8 { s8(...) = delete; };` as having a trivial default constructor (clang `CXX/class/class.union/p1.cpp`, member `m8`). This WP checked the whole matrix against clang 22 and fixed it (spec/sema-class.md §4.3).

- **Matrix**:
  - Constructors: `T(...)`, `T(...) = delete`, `T(int = 0)`, `T(int = 0) = delete`, `T() = delete`, `T() = default`, `template<class...A> T(A...)`, and the copy/move forms `T(const T&, ...) = delete` and `T(T&&, ...) = delete`.
  - Each type is used plain, as a member, as a base, and as a union member.
  - It is checked in C++98 (union-member errors) and in C++11/17/20/26, over 12 traits:
    - `__has_trivial_constructor`, `__has_trivial_copy`;
    - `__is_trivially_constructible`, `__is_trivially_copyable`;
    - `__is_trivial`, `__is_pod`, `__is_literal_type`, `__is_aggregate`;
    - `__is_constructible`, `__is_default_constructible`;
    - `__builtin_is_implicit_lifetime`, `__is_trivially_relocatable`.
  - The constexpr consequences checked: constant default-init, aggregate init, and `__builtin_bit_cast`.
- **What was wrong** (all fixed):
  - **Variadic special members counted as trivial.** `defaulted_is_trivial` did not check for a variadic member (clang `note_nontrivial_variadic`). As a result:
    - `B(...) = delete` had a trivial default constructor, and is_trivial, is_pod and is_literal_type were wrong.
    - `K(const K&, ...) = delete` and `M(M&&, ...) = delete` were trivially copyable, and `__builtin_bit_cast` to `K` was accepted.
    - In C++98, a union member of such a type was not diagnosed (`m8`, plus the copy-ctor case `m11`).
  - **Pre-C++20 aggregates.** Every user-declared constructor made the class a non-aggregate. Before C++20 ([dcl.init.aggr]/1, DR1518), only a user-provided or `explicit` one does. So `struct E { E() = delete; int i; }; constexpr E e{3};` was rejected in C++11–17: a rejects-valid. `__is_aggregate` and `__is_literal_type` were also wrong for the deleted and defaulted forms and their unions.
  - **Pre-C++17 bases.** In C++11/14, a class with a base class was an aggregate; P0017R1 only allows that from C++17.
- **Fix**:
  - `implicit.rs` `defaulted_is_trivial`: a variadic member is not trivial.
  - `Declared::provided_or_explicit_ctor` plus `special_member_bits`: the aggregate bit is cleared by any user-declared constructor from C++20, and only by a user-provided or `explicit` one before C++20.
  - `members.rs` `class_bits`: before C++17, any base class clears the aggregate bit.
- **Tests**:
  - `class.default.ctor/variadic-and-default-arg-not-trivial.cpp`: the trait matrix, C++26.
  - `dcl.init.aggr/deleted-ctor-aggregate-cxx{14,17}.cpp`: run tests with constexpr aggregate init.
  - `class.union/cxx03-deleted-default-ctor.cpp`: C++98 `//@ diag:` checks for `m8`, `m9`, `m11` and `m12`.
  - All of them pass under kiln, clang++-19 and clang++-22.
  - No clang port carried an xfail or skip for `m8`, so there was nothing to flip.
- **Intentional differences**: C++26 union-member traits differ from clang 22 because of P3074 trivial unions (spec/sema-class.md §4.2).
- **Interface changes**:
  - `special/deletion.rs` `fn_info` is now `pub(super)`.
  - New field `Declared::provided_or_explicit_ctor`.
- **Not done** (follow-ups):
  - `N(...) = delete; N() = default;`: clang ORs triviality over the declared members and gives `__has_trivial_constructor` = 1 and `__is_pod` = 1. kiln requires every declared member to be trivial, and gives 0. Switching to OR needs [special]/6 eligibility first, because codegen and `new` skip the constructor call when `TRIVIAL_DEFAULT_CTOR` is set.
  - C++98 cascading error: after an invalid anonymous-union member, kiln also reports "member initializer 'a' does not name a non-static data member or base class" (clang p1.cpp:121). This was already on main.
  - In C++98, the clang warning "deleted function definitions are a C++11 extension" is not issued.
