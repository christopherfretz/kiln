# WP-509 — The control-flow graph and its flow warnings (CFG-a..d of WP-443)

One control-flow graph per analysed function body (clang `CFG` as
`AnalysisBasedWarnings::IssueWarnings` builds it), planned at the end of
the body and built at the first query; every flow-sensitive warning
reads it. Design: docs/design/reachability.md. Rules: spec/sema.md
§7.5 (fall-off, -Wexceptions), §7.5.1 (the graph), §7.5.2 (its
consumers), §7.5.3 (-Winfinite-recursion, -Wmissing-noreturn), §7.10
(-Wunreachable-code*) and "Statement attributes" (-Wimplicit-fallthrough).

- **CFG-a, the builder** (`crates/kiln_sema/src/cfg/`: `mod.rs` model,
  `build.rs` one forward walk, `truth.rs` clang `tryEvaluateBool`,
  `query.rs`, `body.rs` the per-body cache, `dump.rs`). Flat vectors of
  blocks, elements, edges and adjacency, no `Rc`/`RefCell`/`unsafe`;
  the vectors are kept from body to body. `-fkiln-dump-cfg` prints
  clang's `debug.DumpCFG` layout; `-fkiln-cfg-max-elems=N` caps one
  graph (default 33554432), with `-Rkiln-analysis-skipped` for a body
  over it; `-fkiln-cfg-stats` prints each graph's size and build time
  (spec/driver.md). The harness kind `expect: cfg` compares the dump with
  a golden and its shape with clang's (`xtask/src/test/cfg_shape.rs`,
  `cfg-diverges:`; spec/harness.md "cfg tests").
- **Compat 19**: a graph title (`Sema::cfg_title`) prints a closure
  type as `(lambda)` at every `-fclang-compat` level, though
  diagnostics at 19 say `(anonymous class)`, as clang 19 does.
  Reason: clang's analyzer dumps no lambda call operator, so there is no
  clang title to follow. This keeps the `.expected` goldens and the
  graph count that is compared with the oracle the same under
  `KILN_ORACLE=19` (spec/sema.md §7.5.1 "Titles and `-fclang-compat`").
  `stmt.ranged/cfg-ranged.cpp`'s `lambda` covers it.
- **CFG-b**: the runtime-behavior filter (`unreachable_probes`), the
  fall-off analysis behind -Wreturn-type / -Winvalid-noreturn
  (`exit_kinds`, clang `CheckFallThrough`) and the throw checks of
  -Wexceptions (`cfg_escaping_throws`) read the graph.
- **CFG-c**: -Wimplicit-fallthrough and -Wunreachable-code-fallthrough
  (`stmt_flow`) and -Wunreachable-code* (`dead_scan`) read the graph;
  `try_stmt/reach.rs`, the AST walks, is deleted. Every pre-existing
  corpus expectation is unchanged.
- **CFG-d**: -Winfinite-recursion (`infinite_recursion.rs`,
  `Cfg::every_path_hits`; in -Wall) and -Wmissing-noreturn
  (`missing_noreturn.rs`: the graph form in both releases, clang 22's
  inferred `noreturn` for a body that is one throw-expression, which
  also quiets -Wreturn-type in its callers).
- **Hooks for WP-510** (uninitialized values): blocks keep their
  elements in evaluation order, declarations are elements, and every
  block has its predecessor list.

## Perf (harness profile, aarch64 container, -Wall
-Wimplicit-fallthrough -Wunreachable-code-aggressive, so every side
table is built; `-fkiln-cfg-stats`, median of 3, a warm-up body first)

| body | statements | blocks | elems | bytes | build |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| straight line `r += g(x + i);` | 10,000 | 3 | 90,006 | 720 KB | 2.0 ms |
| `switch` with `case i: r += g(i); break;` | 10,000 cases | 10,004 | 70,008 | 1.52 MB | 6.4 ms |
| the same, 20,000 cases | 20,000 cases | 20,004 | 140,008 | 3.04 MB | 12.6 ms |
| `if`/`else` + `for`, 2,500 each | 5,000 | 17,503 | 77,506 | 1.95 MB | 7.1 ms |
| nested `if` 400 deep | 800 | 404 | 4,006 | 71 KB | 0.66 ms |
| nested `if` 1,600 deep | 3,200 | 1,604 | 16,006 | 282 KB | 3.8 ms |
| `tests/stress/cfg-10k-statements.cpp` | 9,990 | 6,331 | 23,649 | 685 KB | 2.9 ms |
| 2,000 small functions (`if`, `while`, 2 returns) | 3 each | 8 | 13 | 676 B | 2.9 µs each |

- Build time per 1k statements: 0.2 ms (straight line) to 1.4 ms
  (branch- and loop-dense), 0.3 ms on the stress body; linear in the
  body for flat and switch-shaped bodies, mildly superlinear in nesting
  depth (×5.8 from 400 to 1,600 levels). The first graph of a process
  costs about 2 ms more (one-time).
- Bytes: an element is 8 B (design budget 16 B); a `Block` is 40 B
  (budget 24 B) and each edge 28 B (12 B edge + 8 B successor + 8 B
  predecessor entry), so 96 B per block on the switch body and 108 B on
  the stress body, edges included. Side tables (flow, probes, case
  blocks) are not counted.
- Whole compile (`-fsyntax-only`, the 10k-case switch): 0.14 s with
  `-w`, 0.15 s with all the flow warnings above; peak RSS 17 to 21 MB.
  The 20k-case switch took 0.64 s with -Wimplicit-fallthrough before
  this WP's fix to the fallthrough walk (it rebuilt each label's block
  list) and 0.34 s after (0.33 s with `-w`).
