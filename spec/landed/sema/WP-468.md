# WP-468 — integer overflow diagnostics and structured-binding specifiers (WP-458 follow-ups)

Extends spec/sema.md §5.28 (`-Winteger-overflow`) and §4.9 (structured
bindings), spec/oven.md (`Oven::fold_for_overflow`,
`Oven::evaluate_call`) and spec/diag.md §7.1 (`group_flag`). Clang 22 is
the oracle; clang 19's behaviour is kept at `-fclang-compat=19` where it
differs.

## `-Winteger-overflow` (`int_overflow.rs`)

- Compound assignment (`+=`, `-=`, `*=`), `++` / `--` (a note only, as
  clang) and an overflow inside a called constexpr body warn, the last at
  the operator in the body, found by a lax Oven fold that wraps and
  records each signed overflow (`Oven::fold_for_overflow`; never
  memoized). `<<=`, `/=`, `%=` get the binary operator's shift-count and
  division-by-zero checks. The warning has the operation's source range
  and no instantiation notes. Tests: `expr.ass/compound-assign-overflow.cpp`,
  `expr.pre/integer-overflow-in-call.cpp`.

## Never a constant expression (`constexpr_body.rs`)

- `{constexpr|consteval} function never produces a constant expression`
  (`sema_constexpr_never_constant`, `-Winvalid-constexpr`, an error by
  default) at the function's name, with the overflow note, for a
  parameterless non-member or static member function whose body always
  overflows; on before C++23, at C++23 and later only with
  `-Winvalid-constexpr` (clang `CheckConstexprFunctionBodies`). The call
  is evaluated by `Oven::evaluate_call`, a potential-constant-expression
  check that reports no `-Wconstant-evaluated`. Tests:
  `dcl.constexpr/never-constant-overflow-cxx{20,23,26}.cpp`.

## Structured-binding specifiers (`struct_bind.rs`, `decl.rs`)

- At `-fclang-compat` >= 22 each specifier not allowed ([dcl.pre]/6) is
  its own `structured binding declaration cannot be declared 'X'` at its
  location; compat 19 keeps clang 19's single combined error.
- [dcl.inline]: `inline` on a block-scope variable is `inline
  declaration of 'x' not allowed in block scope` at `inline` (a
  structured binding names its first binding); the variable is not
  inline. Tests: `dcl.struct.bind/specifiers-each{,-19}.cpp`,
  `dcl.inline/block-scope.cpp`.
