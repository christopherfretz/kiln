# WP-1114 — _Generic selection in C++

kiln now accepts C11 `_Generic` in C++, as clang does: as an extension, warned only under `-pedantic`. Before this WP, the keyword was not parsed in C++ mode (found by WP-954). The behaviour is matched against clang 19 and clang 22.

- **Parse** (spec/parse.md §5, `kiln_parse/src/expr.rs` `parse_generic_selection`).
  - Accepts both the expression operand and clang's type operand (`_Generic(T, ...)`).
  - A `default` association is supported.
  - Warnings: `-Wc11-extensions` on the keyword, and `-Wc2y-extensions` on the type operand.
  - Errors as in clang: the parse errors, and a duplicate `default` with its note.
- **AST** (`kiln_ast/src/expr.rs`).
  - `ExprKind::GenericSelectionExpr` stores the operand, then the `(type, loc, expr)` associations, then the selected association.
  - `ExprArena::selected_operand` is shared with `ChooseExpr`.
  - `generic_control` and `generic_assocs` read the node back.
  - The AST dump prints `_Generic(...)`.
- **Sema** (spec/sema.md "`_Generic` selection", `kiln_sema/src/generic_sel.rs`).
  - The operand is converted while unevaluated.
  - Association-type checks:
    - incomplete: an error at clang 19, a C2y extension at clang 22;
    - not an object type;
    - variably modified;
    - unreachable: a new group, `-Wunreachable-code-generic-assoc`, under `-Wunreachable-code`.
  - Errors for a duplicate type, multiple matches and no match.
  - Dependent selections are rebuilt on substitution (`templ/subst/exprs.rs`).
  - The selected expression is looked through by:
    - `ignore_parens`;
    - the null pointer constant test;
    - bit-field references;
    - the "evaluates to" note on a failed static_assert;
    - the uninitialized-use walk and temporary lifetime extension.
- **Oven / codegen.** The selected association alone is evaluated and emitted, exactly as for `__builtin_choose_expr`.
- **Tests** (`tests/conformance/ext.c11.generic/`, 8 files): `run`, `constexpr`, `templates`, `result`, `diagnostics`, `pedantic`, `incomplete-19` (clang<22) and `incomplete-c2y` (clang>=22).

Not done:
- the `-fdump-ast` clang differential for the node (clang prints its `case`/`default` children);
- module (BMI) serialization of the node.
