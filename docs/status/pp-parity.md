### Performance

These are `cargo xtask bench --filter pp` results on the release build,
5 runs each, measured on aarch64 with WP-015 merged.

| entry | kiln ms | clang ms | time | kiln MB | clang MB | memory |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `pp/headers` | 2342 | 609 | 3.85x | 98.1 | 154.6 | 0.63x |
| `pp/repetition` | 116817 | 792 | **147x** | 293.5 | 210.1 | 1.40x |
| `pp/xmacro` | 3982 | 768 | **5.19x** | 298.6 | 217.9 | 1.37x |

`pp/repetition` and `pp/xmacro` are flagged because they exceed 5x. Both
are in the macro engine (WP-012), not in header search.

- A micro-benchmark repeats `EVAL(args)` calls, where `EVAL` rescans its
  argument 81 times through four nested levels:
  - the time is linear in the number of calls;
  - it is superlinear in the argument's length;
  - 100 calls with 1-token arguments take 0.06 s;
  - 100 calls with 50-token arguments take 6.6 s, 110x for 50x the
    tokens;
  - 400 calls with 50-token arguments take 30.7 s;
  - clang takes 0.01 to 0.07 s on each.
- The suspect is `expand.rs` `relocate()`. Its `Preprocessor::loc_diff`
  call walks the spelling chains of both locations, once per token. That
  walk grows with the argument.
- clang subtracts raw offsets within one `SLocEntry`. kiln's
  `immediate_spelling_loc` mapping is linear within an entry, so the
  same-entry case can be `hi - lo`.
